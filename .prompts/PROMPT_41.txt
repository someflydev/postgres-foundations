PROMPT 41 — Decision-engine catalogs: core_features, extensions, index_patterns, topology_patterns, anti_patterns
=================================================================================================================

## Context

`postgres-foundations`. PROMPT_40 authored industries, data shapes,
and workload patterns. This prompt finishes the catalog set. These
catalogs are the authoritative metadata consumed by the rules
engine (PROMPT_42) and cited throughout the training platform.

Doctrine: every extension entry has "when core is enough" first, then
"workload fit", then "operational cost". No entry without an
explicit "not yet" trigger section.

## Prior work (verify before starting)

- PROMPT_40 three catalogs authored and validating.
- PROMPT_39 schemas directory has `core-feature.schema.json`,
  `extension.schema.json`, `index-pattern.schema.json`,
  `topology-pattern.schema.json`, `anti-pattern.schema.json`.
